What Herbtropia Verified Means

A clear definition of verified, licensed, and certified language across Herbtropia.

Herbtropia Verified

Herbtropia Verified means Herbtropia has reviewed submitted profile details, website/contact information, and credential information when applicable. It does not guarantee outcomes, replace professional judgment, or serve as medical, legal, or clinical endorsement.

Licensed

Licensed means a practitioner has been granted legal permission by a state government or applicable public authority to practice in a regulated field. This may apply to fields such as acupuncture, chiropractic care, naturopathic medicine, massage therapy, counseling, or other regulated services depending on location.

Certified

Certified means a practitioner has completed or passed a training, exam, certificate, or professional program from a private organization, board, school, or association.

Always verify directly

Visitors should confirm credentials directly with the practitioner and relevant licensing or certification organizations where applicable.
