Who Are We?

A calmer way to find holistic wellness support.

Herbtropia is currently growing its Arizona wellness directory. We are inviting holistic practitioners, wellness businesses, event hosts, studios, schools, and community organizations to submit listings for review.

01

Reviewed before publishing

Directory and event submissions are reviewed before they appear publicly, so visitors can browse with more confidence.

02

Built for both sides

Wellness seekers get guided discovery. Practitioners and event hosts get a clearer way to be found by aligned people.

03

Education-first discovery

Herbtropia is for discovery and learning. We do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Founder Note

Built for people tired of wellness rabbit holes.

Herbtropia started from a simple problem: holistic wellness information is scattered everywhere. The goal is to make finding practitioners, events, and trustworthy education easier, clearer, and more community-centered.
